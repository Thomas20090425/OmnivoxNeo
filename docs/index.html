Omnivox Neo

Your college portal, finally readable.

Omnivox Neo signs in to your own Omnivox account and shows you your timetable, your grades and your MIO in an app built this decade. For iPhone, iPad and Mac.

An independent project · Currently in testing · En français

What it does

Your week, at a glance

Today's classes with rooms and times, and two Home Screen widgets — what's up next, and the whole day — so you can check your first room without unlocking anything. The schedule opens first because it is the thing you actually came for.

Grades without the archaeology

Every graded item per course, laid out the way your teacher arranged it, however they arranged it — with or without sections, weighted or not. No digging through nested frames.

MIO that behaves like mail

Read, write, reply, flag, delete. Swipe a message to deal with it. Pick recipients by searching, or from your own classes.

Open it and it is already there

Turn on Face ID and your schedule is on screen before the sign-in finishes, then everything refreshes behind it. With no signal, you still get what is saved on the device, clearly marked as offline rather than silently stale.

Two-factor, handled

Enrol the built-in authenticator once and the verification code fills itself in. Nothing to copy from another app.

Make it yours

Course colours and icons, your own wallpaper, English or French, deadlines mirrored into your calendar if you want them there.

About your data

Omnivox Neo has no server. Your device talks straight to your college's Omnivox address and nowhere else — no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party code, no account with us. Your password lives in your device's keychain behind Face ID, and your grades and messages are never written to storage at all.

The full account of what is kept and what is not is in the Privacy Policy. Your school data itself is governed by your college and by Omnivox, whose policies are linked from there.

The terms you agree to when you sign in are in the End User Licence Agreement — including, in plain words, what this app can change in your account and what happens if it gets it wrong.

Support

Something broken, something confusing, or a feature you want: email yuecheng.ma@dawsoncollege.qc.ca. It helps to say which version you are running — the app shows it under Account, beneath Log Out — and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Things worth knowing first

  • Sign-in sends you to Dawson's site. If your college is waiting on something from you — a student ID photo, an agreement to accept — no app can clear that for you. Omnivox Neo says so and gives you a button to go and sort it out, then a Try Again when you are back.
  • A message needs at least one recipient. Omnivox will not let the last one be removed, so add the new person before taking the old one off.
  • Grades appear when your teacher publishes them. An empty course usually means nothing has been released yet, not that the app missed it.

This is not an official app

Omnivox Neo is made by a student, on his own, and is not built by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Dawson College or by Skytech Communications, who make Omnivox. It signs in with the credentials you give it, to the account you already have, and it reads the same pages you could open in a browser.
