Report a bug, or ask for a feature

This form files an issue against Omnivox Neo. It is read by one person — the student who writes the app — and what happens to it afterwards is public, on the project's issue list.

What you write here becomes public. It is posted as an issue on GitHub, where anyone can read it. Do not include your password, your student number, your grades, or anything else you would not put on a public page.

Before you send it

  • Your name is optional. The form has a name box and leaving it empty is a normal thing to do — the issue then simply says it came from an anonymous contributor. Whatever you type in it is published with the issue, so put in a first name or a nickname rather than anything that identifies you at your college.
  • You do not need a GitHub account, and you are not signing in to anything.
  • Nothing from your college account is attached. This form has no access to your Omnivox session, your timetable, your grades or your messages — it runs in its own window with none of that in it.
  • The form offers to attach a screenshot. Inside the app it can only photograph this form, not the screen you had the problem on, so it is usually worth unticking. Describing what you saw works better.
  • The form itself is in English. The rest of the app is not — this one part is not ours to translate.

What makes a report useful

  • What you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Which screen — schedule, grades, MIO, sign-in.
  • Your app version. It is attached automatically, and it is also shown at the bottom of the Account tab.

Submissions are carried to GitHub by BugDrop, an open-source service that files the issue on your behalf so this app does not have to hold a key that could write to the repository.
