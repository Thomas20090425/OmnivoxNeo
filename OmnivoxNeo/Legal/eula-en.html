The short version, in plain words

This app talks to your college's Omnivox server using your own account. It is not made by your college or by Skytech, it has no permission from either, and it can break. If it breaks it can get things wrong in your real account — a message sent to the wrong person, a message deleted, an authenticator removed. You are the one signed in, so you carry what happens under your login. If that is not a risk you want, use the official Omnivox website or the official Omnivox Mobile app, which is what they are for.

The rest of this page says the same thing at the length the App Store requires. Nothing below takes away a right Quebec or Canadian law gives you — see section 16.

Section 2 says the Application does less than Omnivox. In places it also does more, and that needs saying just as plainly. It already adds a Home Screen widget, a built-in two-factor authenticator, a saved timetable that works without a signal, course colours and wallpapers, and calendar sync. Features may be added later that Omnivox has no equivalent of — reporting a bug, or asking for something to be built, are two that are planned.

None of those are Omnivox features, none of them come from Skytech or your college, and your college does not support them. Three commitments apply to every one of them, now and in future:

  • No added feature will exist in order to collect information about you. That is not what any of them are for, and it will not quietly become what they are for. There is no analytics in this Application, no usage measurement, no profiling, and no advertising, and none will be added.
  • Anything that sends something off your device will be optional, off until you switch it on, and honest about what it sends. It will tell you what leaves, and where it goes, before it goes — not in a settings screen you were supposed to find. It will send the least that makes the feature work, and nothing collected for one purpose will be reused for another.
  • It will be written down before it ships. Any new feature that touches your information will appear in this Agreement and in the Privacy Policy, with the version date moved, and you will be asked to accept the revised terms — not told afterwards.

Being unofficial is not a reason to be unclear. It is the reason to be clearer: you have no institution standing behind this Application, so the only thing you have is what it tells you about itself, and that has to be true.

5. What the Application can do to your account, and the risk you accept

The Application does not only read. To be useful it also acts in your account: with your instruction it can send MIO messages, delete them, flag and file them, choose recipients, mark items read, write entries into your device calendar, and enrol or remove a two-factor authentication method. It does these by driving your college's own web pages automatically, on the live portal, and some of them cannot be undone.

Because the Application interprets pages that Skytech and your college may change without notice, a defect in it — a bug, a mistaken assumption, or a change it misreads — can cause changes in your real account that you did not intend. Realistically that includes:

  • a message sent to the wrong recipient, or sent when you did not mean to send one;
  • a message deleted, flagged or filed that you wanted kept;
  • a two-factor authenticator enrolled, replaced or removed, which can lock you out of your own account until your college restores it;
  • calendar entries created, changed or deleted;
  • information shown wrongly, incompletely or out of date — a grade, a deadline, a room, a time — on which you might act;
  • a session left in a state your college's system rejects, requiring you to sign in again or contact your college.

You accept and assume these risks. You agree to verify any consequential action in the official Omnivox portal.
